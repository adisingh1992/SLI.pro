# SECTION 4: HEARTBEAT PROMPTS (8 per track, voice-calibrated)

---

## Undergraduate

1. "What did you work on this week that you'd want to tell someone about?"
2. "Was there a moment this week where you surprised yourself — with how much you knew, or how much you didn't?"
3. "Who did you learn something from this week — and what was it?"
4. "One word for your week. Go. And then tell me the story behind it."
5. "Did you end up talking to anyone new this week — at school, online, anywhere? What did you talk about?"
6. "What's one thing you did this week that felt effortless?"
7. "Was there a conversation this week that stuck with you? What about it?"
8. "What moment from this week do you keep replaying in your head — good or bad?"

---

## Early Career

1. "Did anything unexpected grab your attention at work this week? What was it?"
2. "Did your SLI come up this week — in a conversation, an email, or just in your head?"
3. "What did you do this week that someone thanked you for?"
4. "What's one thing about your work this week that you're still thinking about?"
5. "What conversation this week gave you energy?"
6. "Was there a moment this week where you felt like you were in your zone — doing the thing you do best?"
7. "Anything show up on your plate this week that wasn't supposed to be there?"
8. "What's one thing you explained to someone this week that they found helpful?"

---

## Mid-Career

1. "What's a call you made this week where your experience really mattered?"
2. "What did you see this week that the rest of your organization hasn't noticed yet?"
3. "Did your positioning come up this week? How did you describe yourself?"
4. "What's something happening in your industry right now that you have a strong opinion about?"
5. "What conversation shifted your thinking this week?"
6. "What did you delegate this week — and what did you keep?"
7. "Is there someone in your organization whose influence you observed this week? What were they doing?"
8. "What would you tell your team about this week that they don't already know?"

---

## Executive

1. "What strategic pattern did you notice this week that nobody else seems to see?"
2. "What did you learn this week from someone who reports to you?"
3. "What opinion did you form this week that you haven't shared with anyone yet?"
4. "Was there a moment this week where you thought about what you're building that will outlast you?"
5. "What decision did you make this week that you'd make differently if you had more time?"
6. "Was there a moment this week where you could feel your influence landing — or not landing?"
7. "What's one thing happening in your industry that changes how you think about the next two years?"
8. "Did anything come up this week — feedback, a reaction, a result — that told you something about how you're leading?"

---

## Entrepreneur

1. "What did you learn about your market this week that you didn't know before?"
2. "What's a conversation from this week that stuck with you — easy or hard?"
3. "Did anything land on your plate this week that you're second-guessing taking on?"
4. "If someone asked you 'why are you the right person to build this?' today, what would you say?"
5. "Who did you try to influence this week — and how did it go?"
6. "What's one part of your company that didn't get your attention this week — and should it have?"
7. "If your team could ask you for one thing right now, what do you think it would be?"
8. "What signal from your customers or market surprised you this week?"

---

## Career Returner

1. "How did you describe yourself to someone new this week?"
2. "What did you do this week that reminded you of what you're good at?"
3. "What came back faster than you expected this week? And anything still feel like it needs warming up?"
4. "Was there a moment this week where the gap felt like an advantage, not a liability?"
5. "What's one thing you noticed about your industry that's changed since you were last in it?"
6. "Who reached out to you this week, or who did you reach out to?"
7. "What did you read or learn this week that made you feel more ready?"
8. "What would you tell someone else who's re-entering the workforce after a break?"

# SECTION 5: NEW POPULATION PROMPTS (from UX Addendum)

These are the research-informed prompts for the three new populations. Heartbeat-appropriate prompts are marked. Prompts marked *synthesis-cycle* should only be used at monthly/quarterly cadence, not in the weekly rotation.

---

## Entrepreneur (Fundraising) — 10 prompts

**Design principles:** Process-oriented, not emotional. Avoid valuation or term-sheet language. Lower-disclosure prompts first.

EF-01: "What's one thing an investor pushed back on this week that you're still turning over?" *(heartbeat — requires event-sensitivity flag: only when Wing knows they had a meeting)*
Signal: pitch gap identification vs. emotional reaction

EF-02: "Is there a difference between how you describe your company to investors and how you describe it to friends?" *(heartbeat)*
Signal: narrative bifurcation

EF-03: "Quick — what are you good at that has nothing to do with your company?" *(heartbeat)*
Signal: self-complexity baseline

EF-04: "What was the hardest question you got in your last investor meeting, and what was your first internal reaction before you answered?" *(heartbeat — requires event-sensitivity flag)*
Signal: defensiveness vs. curiosity

EF-05: "If your company pivoted completely away from [current focus], what would that mean about you?" *(synthesis-cycle only)*
Signal: construal distance

EF-06: "What did you learn from the last investor who said no — about them, about your pitch, or about the market?" *(heartbeat — requires event-sensitivity flag)*
Signal: prosocial vs. avoidant rejection pattern

EF-07: "Right now, are you more worried about not raising enough or about raising from the wrong people?" *(heartbeat)*
Signal: fear architecture

EF-08: "What's the part of your fundraising story that you believe most — and the part you're least sure about?" *(heartbeat)*
Signal: narrative confidence map

EF-09: "Did anything an investor said this week change how you actually think about your business — not just your pitch?" *(heartbeat — requires event-sensitivity flag)*
Signal: whether investor feedback reaches private decision-making

EF-10: "When you imagine your company in five years, are you running it or have you built something that runs without you?" *(synthesis-cycle)*
Signal: Rich-vs-King diagnostic

## Entrepreneur (Scaling) — 10 prompts

**Design principles:** Delegation and control as core themes. Surface delegation resistance as identity signal, not quality-control issue. Name the grief without pathologizing.

ES-01: "What's a decision you made alone this week that, in six months, you'd want someone else on your team to own?" *(heartbeat)*
Signal: RACI bottleneck detection

ES-02: "What decisions in your company still reflect something only you would have chosen?" *(heartbeat)*
Signal: imprint recognition

ES-03: "What part of how you used to work do you miss most?" *(heartbeat)*
Signal: identity grief

ES-04: "Did you step back into something this week that your team was supposed to own? What pulled you in?" *(heartbeat)*
Signal: delegation resistance

ES-05: "What would it mean about you if your team's version was good enough?" *(synthesis-cycle only)*
Signal: core delegation identity question

ES-06: "What's the last thing about running your company that actually surprised you — not a problem, but something you didn't expect?" *(heartbeat)*
Signal: curiosity trajectory

ES-07: "If you stepped away from your company for a month, what would break?" *(heartbeat)*
Signal: single-point-of-failure mapping

ES-08: "What's one thing you're doing today that you were also doing when the company had 3 people? Should you still be doing it?" *(heartbeat)*
Signal: role evolution tracking

ES-09: "Who on your team most disagrees with you right now — and how do you feel about that?" *(heartbeat)*
Signal: constructive confrontation readiness

ES-10: Monthly visual overlap measure — "You" and "Your Company" as overlapping circles. *(monthly calibration)*
Signal: fusion trajectory over time

## Executive — 8 prompts

**Design principles:** Temporal anchoring and behavioral specificity over self-assessment. Disclosure framed as optional. Two-step pairing when possible.

EX-01: "What work thought keeps coming back to you — the one that shows up when you're not at your desk?" *(heartbeat)*
Signal: what occupies mental bandwidth beyond office hours

EX-02: "Walk me through your last decision where the outcome was different from what you expected." *(heartbeat)*
Signal: behavioral specificity

EX-03: "Any conversations on your mind that you haven't had yet?" *(heartbeat)*
Signal: confrontation avoidance

EX-04: "If your team could tell you one thing anonymously, what do you think they'd say?" *(heartbeat)*
Signal: perception-gap awareness

EX-05: "You've described [N] strong weeks in a row. What's the one thing you'd change if you could?" *(reveal-trigger — only fires on uniformly positive pattern)*
Signal: incongruence surfacing

EX-06: "If someone shadowed you for a day this week, what would they say you spent the most time on — and would you agree with them?" *(heartbeat)*
Signal: gap between self-model and observable behavior

EX-07: "What's a skill-related advantage you have that you rarely talk about in professional settings?" *(heartbeat — paired with "no one sees this but you" framing)*
Signal: voluntary disclosure

EX-08: "Who in your organization has the most influence that doesn't show up on the org chart — and how do you navigate them?" *(heartbeat)*
Signal: power literacy and political acuity
